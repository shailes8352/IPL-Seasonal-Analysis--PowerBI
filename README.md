# 🏏 IPL Seasonal Analysis Dashboard (2008–2024)

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze seasonal performance, team stats, and player metrics across 16+ seasons of the Indian Premier League (IPL).

---

## 📌 Dashboard Preview
![Dashboard Preview](Screenshot%202026-08-09%20004011.png)

---

## 📊 Key Insights & KPIs
* *Core KPIs:* Total Matches (1095), Total Runs (3,47,756), Total Wickets, Total Fours, and Total Sixes.
* *Top Performers:* Highlights top 5 batsmen and top 5 bowlers across seasons.
* *Team Stats:* Visualized Top 5 most successful teams based on match wins.
* *Interactive Slicer:* Custom season filter to dynamically switch metrics by year.
